Study Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Study Tracker is a free personal study planner. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to:

  • see your basic profile (name and email address) so it can show who is signed in;
  • create and use only the files it creates in your Google Drive (Google's drive.file permission). The app cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter — goals, study hours, tasks, habits and timer sessions — is saved in a Google Sheet named “Study Tracker” in your own Google Drive, and temporarily on your device so the app works offline. There is no separate server or database. The app's owner cannot see your data.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else. It is only exchanged between your device and Google's servers.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the app (Settings → Sign out) to remove the copy stored on that device.
  • Delete the “Study Tracker” sheet from your Google Drive to remove your data permanently.
  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner at the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
